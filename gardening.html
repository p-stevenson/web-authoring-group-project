What is Gardening?

Gardening is the practice of growing and caring for plants. Gardening involves nurturing plants by providing the right balance of soil, water, sunlight, and care. People garden for many reasons like growing their own food or making a space more pretty or even just as a hobby.

For basic materials and tools you might need when starting out, you might want to get yourself a hand trowel for digging small holes, some gardening gloves and a watering can or hose if you don't have one already.

Gardening for Beginners

This video will help explain the basics for gardening in case you need another visual guide. There are many videos like this so please research if you are interested.

Some basic tips for beginner gardeners are to start small (maybe with just a small patch) and choosing plants that are easy to grow such as mint or hardy flowers. It's very important that you don't overwater your flowers either, as that could stifle their growth. Gardening is very flexible so you can do it indoors or in a yard or even on a balcony. Over time you'll learn what works best for you.

Start Your Journey

Everyone's a beginner at first, which is why it's important to understand what sort of goals you want to achieve with gardening, even as just a hobby. Below are some steps you can take when starting out, then getting more familiar with the hobby, and then taking on some advanced gardening projects once you feel comfortable.

1

BEGINNER

You will need basic tools such as a hand trowel, watering can, and gardening gloves. A few pots or a small garden bed should be enough for a beginner gardener to practice growing some plants. Start with easy plants such as mint, basil and marigolds. It will be trial and error so be patient and avoid overwatering.

2

ENTHUSIAST

Now that you're building skill, you can start understanding different soil types and using good compost. You will have to learn how to deal with pests as well. Some simple projects you can start with could be a small vegetable patch or a herb garden.

3

EXPERT

At an expert level, you're thinking long-term for your garden. Develop skill in propagation of your garden and design a garden layout that suits you. Specialize in a specific type of gardening such as greenhouses and landscaping. Don't forget to upgrade your equipment too!
