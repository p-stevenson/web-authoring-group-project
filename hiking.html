Loughcrew Video

Set off from the amazing Loughcrew Cairns, and scale the grassy hills that surround the area. The walk covers 3 to 4 kilometres of trail, is graded as a medium challenge, and can take between 40 and 60 minutes to complete.

It is worth noting that there are guided tours available during the summer months, but the path is uneven and steep, so it will not suit walkers with mobility issues.

Walking Spots

These are the main locations from the original hiking page, kept here as the hiking copy and styled to match the other subpages.

Rathbeggan Lakes, Batterstown Co. Meath

Set in the gently sloping valley of the River Tolka, this beautiful springfed stillwater complex could be miles from anywhere. Hard to believe it is less than 10 minutes from Dublin's busy M50. With a choice of 4 different fishing lakes and a selection of other family friendly activities on offer, there is something for everyone to enjoy.

Littlewood Forest Loop

The Littlewood loop trail takes you through a mixed conifer and broadleaf woodland. It starts and ends at the car park. The trees along the route include oak, ash, alder, sycamore, beech, horse chestnut, hazel, rowan, holly, hawthorn, blackthorn, and elm, with display boards that highlight the variety of flora and fauna found in the woodland.

Hill of Tara

The Hill of Tara is an archaeological site situated between Navan and Dunshaughlin in County Meath. Containing a number of ancient monuments, it was the seat of the High King of Ireland and the most important centre of political and religious power.

Girley Bog

Girley Bog Eco Walk is a 3.5 mile, 5.6 km waymarked national loop. It covers varying landscape of forest and bogland. The terrain is flat and uneven and walking boots are recommended. It is one of the few remaining raised bogs in County Meath and includes a number of interpretive panels along the route.

Loughcrew

What will you see? Breath-taking scenery across County Meath and the three cairns at Loughcrew, which date back some 5,000 years. There is a parking area at the main entrance and you start the trail from there, making it a strong option when you want a more dramatic walk.
