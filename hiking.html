HIKING

A good excuse to get out, clear your head, and walk farther than planned.

What is Hiking?

Hiking can be as simple as a marked trail and a decent pair of boots. For some people it is about the view, for others it is the quiet bit in the middle when the phone stops mattering. You do not need to head straight for mountains either; woodland paths, coastal walks, and local loops all count.

To start off, keep it easy. Wear shoes with grip, bring water, a light layer, and something to eat, then pick a route that is shorter than you think you need. The goal of the first few walks is not to look impressive, it is to finish thinking you would go again.

Start Your Journey

Most people start with local loops, then keep stretching it from there.

1

BEGINNER

Start with marked routes, decent weather, and a small bag with water, a snack, and a rain layer. Learn how your boots feel, how fast you actually walk, and when you need to turn round instead of pushing on.

2

ENTHUSIAST

After a few good walks you start choosing routes with more climb, rougher ground, or longer distance. That is usually when people sort better gear, check forecasts properly, and get fussier about maps.

3

EXPERT

More experienced hikers plan around daylight, terrain, weather, and the slowest person in the group. They know pacing matters, shortcuts are often a bad idea, and a turned-back walk is still a good walk.
