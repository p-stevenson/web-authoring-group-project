TRAVEL

Sometimes it is the airport buzz, sometimes it is just getting properly lost for a day.

Why do people fall in love with travel?

People get hooked on travel for different reasons. Some like the planning, some like landing somewhere that smells and sounds completely different, and some just want a break from their usual routine. Even a short trip can stick in your head for years if the timing is right.

Your first few trips do not need to be ambitious. Pick somewhere manageable, sort the passport and visa bits early, and leave enough time for the boring airport stuff. Once that side is handled, the fun part starts to feel a lot less frantic.

Start Your Journey

It usually starts with one manageable trip, then gets easier every time.

1

BEGINNER

Start with one place, one booking plan, and a budget you can actually follow. Check your passport, baggage allowance, airport transfer, and somewhere to sleep before you go, then leave yourself more time than you think you need.

2

ENTHUSIAST

After a few trips you stop overpacking and stop panicking at departure boards. You get better at spotting decent flight times, using local transport, and leaving a bit of room in the plan for random finds.

3

EXPERT

Seasoned travellers still plan the dull stuff carefully, they just do it faster. They think about value, energy, seasons, and how much moving around is actually worth it, then leave enough slack in the schedule for the unexpected.
